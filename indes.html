About

I study how a power system can run on renewable energy alone, and what it would take to connect such grids across countries and continents.

My PhD at VNIT Nagpur is part of the Indo-European project Global Electricity Grid: The Future of Earth, funded by the Ministry of Education, India, in collaboration with the University of Liège, DTU and CentraleSupélec. Alongside resource assessment and grid design, I build metaheuristic optimisation algorithms for optimal power flow and reactive power dispatch, and I carry out power system studies on real network data, including real-time simulation on Typhoon HIL and a real-time transmission line simulator.

Outside research I write and blog, invest in the stock market, and spend time on photography, music and audiobooks.

Global Electricity Interconnection with 100% RE Generation

PhD research scholar

  1. 1Context

    A 100% renewable supply for India, and a grid that crosses continents, are unproven at scale. The work sits in the Indo-European project “Global Electricity Grid: The Future of Earth”, funded by the Ministry of Education, India, with Prof. Damien Ernst (University of Liège), Prof. Spyros Chatzivasileiadis (DTU) and Dr. Jing Dai (CentraleSupélec).

  2. 2What I did

    Built a Critical Time Window Framework to site onshore and offshore wind and solar PV in India, and assessed the resource with Weibull distributions and a temporal variability index. Designed an enhanced Indian interconnected grid at 100% RE with HVDC lines, costed it in GBOML including HVDC cost and losses, then selected countries and HVDC routes for a global grid on RE potential, demand, policy and political stability.

  3. 3Outcome

    A costed pathway to 100% renewable supply in India and a first global-grid design, plus a three-month visiting research stay at the University of Liège.

Optimal Power Flow with an Enhanced Butterfly Optimization Algorithm

MTech researcher

  1. 1Context

    Standard optimal power flow solvers struggle with non-convex, multi-objective problems.

  2. 2What I did

    Hybridised the Butterfly Optimization Algorithm with non-uniform mutation, an EMA balanced state and an elite strategy; validated it on classical and CEC benchmark functions; then applied it to single- and multi-objective stochastic OPF and security-constrained OPF on the IEEE 30-bus, IEEE 118-bus and Indian 62-bus systems.

  3. 3Outcome

    A solver that handles single- and multi-objective OPF variants on standard and Indian test systems.

  • Optimal power flow
  • Metaheuristics
  • Benchmarking

Power System Studies on Real Grid Data

Analyst and designer

  1. 1Context

    A real distribution network needs validated load flow, known fault levels and a renewable retrofit.

  2. 2What I did

    Ran load flow on the IEEE 14-bus system and real Nagpur data for voltage magnitude and angle, active and reactive power at every bus, and line flows; ran fault analysis for LG, LL, LLG, LLL and LLLG faults to size relays and circuit breakers; then designed a hybrid wind-plus-solar grid on the same network.

  3. 3Outcome

    A validated study basis for protection design and a working hybrid renewable grid design.

  • Load flow
  • Fault analysis
  • Hybrid RE
